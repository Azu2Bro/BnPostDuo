# BnPostDuo original image availability

## Public dataset

The official processed BnPostDuo CSV dataset is available on [Mendeley Data, Version 2](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/6678wc3bs7/2) and contains **4,708 records** across eight classes. The public CSV package contains captions, raw and curated OCR text, labels, pHash, and image dimensions.

## Original Facebook images

The original Facebook images were used during dataset construction for OCR extraction, annotation, and quality control. **These images are not part of the published dataset.** The research team does not have the necessary rights to redistribute the original third-party images and does not make them available.

The released records preserve image-derived textual information and metadata but do not provide image pixels. Researchers who wish to conduct image-only or direct image–text experiments must obtain any required original images independently and lawfully, in accordance with applicable copyright, privacy, and platform requirements.

## Dataset identifiers and metadata

The `image_id` field provides a record identifier; it is not an image download link. The `phash` field is a perceptual image hash and cannot reconstruct an image. The eight categories are Educational, Entertainment, Marketing, Motivational, News, Political, Religious, and Social Awareness.

## Repository roles

- **Mendeley Data:** Public, citable Version 2 CSV dataset (4,708 records).
- **GitHub:** Documentation, reproducibility scripts, validation reports, and the image-availability statement.

This policy does not change the contents or license of the existing Mendeley Data deposit.
