# Data and content rights

The repository's MIT LICENSE applies to original software scripts only.

The official BnPostDuo CSV dataset is distributed through [Mendeley Data Version 2](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/6678wc3bs7/2) (4,708 records) and is subject to the license and permissions specified for that version. GitHub does not relicense that dataset or third-party Facebook content.

Original Facebook images are **not redistributed in the public Mendeley Data or GitHub files**, including via embedded JSON, Base64, or other reversible representations. Researchers may request access through the [BnPostDuo Google Form](https://forms.gle/wKSdaqRD4rpT5T6f7). Requests are reviewed; a private Google Drive link may be supplied **only to approved researchers and only where the team has the authority to share the relevant images**. The link is not public and access is not automatic. Perceptual hashes do not reconstruct
those images. Captions, OCR text, or identifiers may themselves implicate
third-party rights or privacy obligations.

Figures and documentation are provided for reference; no additional license
is granted for their reuse unless their authors separately authorize it.

The 480-row category-vote data have not been published in this GitHub repository.
If released after author review, a votes-only extract should avoid image
identifiers, URLs, source text, and other unneeded source metadata.

Current Mendeley Data Version 2: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/6678wc3bs7/2 (4,708 records).
Historical Version 1: https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/6678wc3bs7/1 (4,800 records).
